FAQs

Questions customers often ask

Need a specific answer? Call or WhatsApp with your appliance make, model and suburb.

Do you repair both home and commercial appliances?

Yes. Appliance Solutions accepts enquiries for home appliances and selected commercial equipment, including commercial refrigerators, cold rooms and ice makers. Share the equipment details so service suitability can be confirmed.

Do you offer same-day appliance repairs?

Same-day service may be available depending on your suburb, technician availability, the appliance and parts required. Submit a request or call to check current availability.

Do you provide onsite repairs?

Onsite service is available by arrangement across the service area. Include your suburb and appliance fault when requesting a quote.

Which appliance brands do you repair?

We handle enquiries for major appliance brands. Include the brand and model number in your request so we can confirm whether we can assist with your specific unit.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form on this page or WhatsApp 072 210 0035. Tell us the appliance type, symptoms, brand/model if known and your suburb.
